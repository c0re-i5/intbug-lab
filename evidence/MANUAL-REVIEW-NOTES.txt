----------------------------------------------------------------------
CORRECTED (this slice): wu_zero_stream probe artifact
----------------------------------------------------------------------

PRIOR claim (widen slice): httpd / envoy / nghttpd FAIL on wu_zero_stream
(= "accepted zero stream WINDOW_UPDATE").

ROOT CAUSE: our probe sent GET with END_STREAM, drained the response, then
sent WINDOW_UPDATE(stream=1, incr=0) on a closed/half-closed stream. Several
stacks silently ignore illegal WUs in that state. That is a *probe artifact*,
not an RFC-conformance finding.

FIX (this slice): open stream with HEADERS flags=END_HEADERS only (no
END_STREAM), send WU(0) immediately — matches h2spec http2/6.9/2.

RESULT after fix (framing capture + battery + h2spec):
  nginx-1.31.8 / nginx-1.31.9 / httpd-2.4.71 / nghttpd → PASS
      (GOAWAY PROTOCOL_ERROR). Byte-identical WU frame:
      00 00 04 08 00 00 00 00 01 00 00 00 00
  caddy-2.10 / go-nethttp → ANOMALY (RST_STREAM PROTOCOL_ERROR on stream;
      connection kept). h2spec still ✔-passes these.
  envoy-1.32 → ANOMALY (hard close, no GOAWAY). h2spec ✔-passes
      (treats connection closed as rejection).

h2spec (bin/h2spec v2.6.0) http2/6.9/1 + http2/6.9/2: 2/2 passed on ALL
seven h2 targets. Evidence under evidence/zero-wu/.

Honesty: the earlier "shared FAIL across Apache/Envoy/nghttpd" is WITHDRAWN
as a conformance claim. Remaining ANOMALYs are stream-scoped / hard-close
rejection styles, still NOT CVE claims.
